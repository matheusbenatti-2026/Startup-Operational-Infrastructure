How to use this checklist

  1. Assess — Complete the checklist with your leadership team. Be honest about what actually happens day to day.
  2. Prioritize — Identify the three biggest gaps affecting execution, accountability, or delivery.
  3. Assign ownership — Give each gap one accountable owner, a specific deliverable, and a deadline.
  4. Review in 30 days — Check progress and repeat the assessment quarterly or after significant growth.

A simple rule

If a responsibility has no owner, a deliverable has no clear expectation, or a recurring process exists only in someone’s head, the startup has an operational gap.

↗

The goal isn’t a large corporate structure. It’s just enough structure to make execution predictable, accountability visible, and growth sustainable.

Build only what your business needs now. Improve it as the business grows.
